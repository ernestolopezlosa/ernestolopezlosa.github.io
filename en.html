About

I am an Associate Professor of Economic History at the University of the Basque Country. My work combines archival sources and historical analysis to study material living conditions in early modern Spain and the transformation of Basque and Spanish fisheries.

I teach in Basque, Spanish and English. I have led research projects on work, real wages and consumption in preindustrial Spain, and undertaken research visits at Duke University, New York University and the University of Gothenburg.

Contact: ernesto.lopez@ehu.eus
