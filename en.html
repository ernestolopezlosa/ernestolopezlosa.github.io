Prices, wages, living standards and the history of fisheries in Spain.

I am an Associate Professor of Economic History at the University of the Basque Country. My research examines how people lived and worked in preindustrial Spain, and how institutions shaped the use of fisheries resources.

01 / Research

Research

Two connected areas of research bring archival evidence to questions about welfare, markets and resource management.

01

Prices, wages and living standards

Reconstructing price and real wage series, living costs and material conditions in early modern Spain. Recent work examines European divergence and the historical meaning of a “respectable” standard of living.

Preindustrial Spain · Labour · Consumption
02

Fisheries, institutions and environment

The economic history of Basque and Spanish fisheries, focusing on guilds, access rights, technology, markets and the adaptation of fishing communities.

Fisheries · Common resources · Maritime history
