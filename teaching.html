Teaching

Economic History in Basque, Spanish and English at the University of the Basque Country’s Faculty of Economics and Business.

Courses

My teaching centres on Economic History and the Economic History of Spain. I have also taught postgraduate courses on data processing for historians.

  • Economic History.
  • Economic History of Spain in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries.
  • Demography and population.
  • Data processing for historians, at postgraduate level.

Academic coordination

I have coordinated the Economic History course and the Marketing degree programme. I was Vice Dean for Basque Language and Students in the Faculty of Economics and Business from 2009 to 2017.
